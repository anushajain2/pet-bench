# Data attribution and terms

Original experimental data: Norton-Baker B, Komp E, Gado JE, et al. (2025).
Machine Learning-Guided Identification of PET Hydrolases from Natural Diversity.
ACS Catalysis 15 (18), 16070–16083. https://doi.org/10.1021/acscatal.5c03460

Workbook: `cs5c03460_si_002.xlsx`, ACS Figshare article 30044876, file 57638729.
https://acs.figshare.com/articles/dataset/30044876
https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/57638729

The Figshare record identifies this supplement as **CC BY-NC 4.0**:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
The code's MIT license does not relicense the experimental data. Source and
derived measurement tables are attributed noncommercial research materials.
Changes: condition filtering, replicate averaging, identical-sequence collapsing,
and addition of model predictions, similarity measures, and evaluation metadata.
The authors do not endorse this project. No new biological measurements were made.
